[Title]
zzCadencePlus - Cadence, Avg, Max, Lap

[Description]
A cadence data field with big, easy-to-read digits. Shows your current cadence together with a second value of your choice, and colors it when you are above or below your average.
Built with the TitilliumWeb font for better readability on the move.
A rebuilt version of zzCadence.

- Needs a cadence sensor.

[Second value]
- Average cadence
- Max cadence
- Lap average (calculated in the app, coasting at 0 rpm is left out, resets on lap)
- None

[Colors]
- Current cadence changes color when it is more than 5% above or below the average (or the lap average in lap mode).
- Pick separate colors for high and low, for day and night.
- The background follows your device setting (white or black).

[Layouts, picked automatically]
- One slot of the 10-field layout: cadence icon and second value on top, large current cadence below.
- Wide slots: zzCadencePlus title with the active mode (AVG / MAX / LAP) on top, both values side by side at the same size below.
- Same typeface, sizes and layout as the other Plus data fields, so they look consistent side by side.

[Supported devices]
Edge 540 / 550 / 840 / 850 / 1040 / 1050 / 1060

Change settings in the Garmin Connect IQ app or Garmin Express.
